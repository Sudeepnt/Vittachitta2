Questions to ask
before signing

With anyone, including us. Handing over your company's finances comes with a few traps. Better to know them going in.

01

Who it is for

Who is VitthaChittha for?

Any founder who wants full visibility of their company's finances at every stage, from the idea to the exit.

How is this different from an accounting firm?

An accounting firm completes defined tasks such as filings and returns. We build and run the financial architecture itself, represent you to everyone connected to it, and answer for the outcomes.

Do you replace our accountant or our advisors?

Not necessarily. Where good people are in place, they keep their roles and we make sure their work reaches its outcome.

We have not incorporated yet. Is it too early?

No. It is the best time. The structure you choose on day zero is the hardest thing to change later.

02

Working together

Where do we start?

With a discovery call, then a Financial Health Audit. The audit tells us both which platform the company needs first.

What happens when we outgrow the Operating platform?

That is the plan. As volume grows, the company builds its own finance team. We move up to the Founder's Office and oversee everyone who does the work. Confirm

We already have a finance team. Where do you start?

At the Founder's Office. We put what exists into order, take over the conversations on your behalf, and hold each person's work to its outcome.

What is a Forward Deployed Finance Operator?

A member of our team who works inside your company to learn how money and financial information really move through it, and who stays to check what the system says.

03

Costs

What does the discovery call cost?

Nothing. It is complimentary.

What does the Financial Health Audit cost?

It is a fixed fee, scoped on the discovery call to the size and stage of the company and agreed before any work begins.

Do we pay before we know what is wrong?

No. The audit comes first and stands on its own. You receive a rating for each area, a red-flag register and a 90-day plan, whether or not we work together afterwards.

04

Chittha and your data

Is Chittha available today?

Chittha is in development with a small group of founders. The Operating platform and the Founder's Office are what we do for companies today, and they are what Chittha is built on.

How is Chittha different from other AI finance tools?

Most tools start with the software and hope the data is good. Chittha starts with books our team keeps current and an office that owns every finance conversation. Then our own operators go inside the company and map it, before any intelligence is switched on. The intelligence is the last step, not the first.

Does Chittha act on its own?

No. It watches, understands and prepares. Nothing is posted, paid, filed or sent until a person on our team has approved it.

Who operates Chittha?

Our team does. Your people do not have to learn a new tool. They ask, and we answer with what it shows, checked by a person.

Where do our systems and records live?

Inside your company's own architecture. We build there, not on our side.

    One model of the whole company.

    Most finance data sits in separate tools that do not know about each other. The ontology joins them, so a change in one place is understood everywhere it matters.

    01

    Objects

    The real things in the business: an invoice, a customer, a loan, a filing, an employee, a share certificate. Each has one record, kept in step with its sources.

    02

    Links

    How those things connect. This invoice belongs to that contract, which belongs to that customer, which carries that credit term, which moves that forecast.

    03

    Actions

    What may be done to each object, by whom, and with whose approval. Every action is recorded against the object it changed.

    Watch. Understand. Prepare.

    Chittha runs as a loop, all day and all night. The fourth step belongs to a person: nothing is posted, paid, filed or sent without a human yes.

    01

    Watch

    Bank feeds, ledgers, tax and company-law portals, contracts, inboxes and chats. It raises only what needs attention.

    02

    Understand

    Each signal is matched to the objects it touches in the ontology: the customer, the contract, the filing, the forecast.

    03

    Prepare

    It drafts the entry, the reconciliation, the filing working, the reminder or the report, with the evidence attached.

    04

    A person approves

    An operator reviews, corrects and signs. Their decision is written back, so the next answer is better than the last.

    To every founder,

    You started a company to build something. Nobody starts one to chase a TDS challan, or to learn what a valuation report is on the night before a board meeting.


    Yet from the day your company is registered, you are answerable for every rupee in it. The people who help you are good at their part: the accountant, the company secretary, the lawyer, the auditor. But each of them holds one piece, and the only person holding all the pieces is you.


    We think that is the wrong design. A founder should be able to ask any question about the company's money and get a true answer the same day. Getting there is hard. The data is scattered, it arrives late, and most of it lives in people's heads and inboxes. Software alone does not fix that, and neither does one more advisor.


    So we do it in order. First we build the systems, and our own team runs them while the company is young. Then we become your office for finance. We speak for you to every auditor, banker, lawyer and investor, and we hold each of them to the outcome they promised. Then our operators go deep into how your company really works. Only after that do we add intelligence, because intelligence built on bad data is confidently wrong.


    We work for the founder. When the numbers are good, you will know why. When they are bad, you will hear it from us first, early enough to act.


    That is the whole promise. Everything else on this site is how we keep it.
