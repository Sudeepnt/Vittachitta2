      Our thesis

      The founder is the integrator.

      Every company has a finance function. Almost no founder has a finance office. Nine findings. One answer.

      Scroll
      01

      The load is real.

      69,233 compliances. Two in five carry a jail term. Most sit with the states.

      ORF & TeamLease RegTech, Jailed for Doing Business, 2022.

      02

      More founders carry it every year.

      502 recognised startups in 2016. Over 2.23 lakh by March 2026. Each inherits the full load on day one.

      DPIIT via KNN, Indian Bureaucracy, Bombay Chamber, Dinamalar, Inc42.

      03

      The founder pays in hours.

      36% of a 45.5-hour week goes to small admin. Sixteen hours, every week, broken into pieces.

      Time etc & Censuswide survey of 251 growth-business founders, 2023.

      04

      The gaps, not the experts.

      Each expert owns a slice. Each is liable for that slice. Nobody owns the whole.

      • BYJU'STwo statutory auditors resigned in about a year.
      • GoMechanicAn investor-commissioned audit ended the round.
      • VerSeAuditors flagged weak internal controls on FY24.

      TechCrunch, Outlook Startup, Inc42. Causes differ; some remain contested.

      05

      Capital got choosier.

      Funding fell 58% from 2022 to 2025. Rounds fell 39% in a year. Diligence got sharper.

      Tracxn via Outlook Startup and The Mainstream; Axial Dead Deal Report 2024.

      06

      Cracks are cheap. Failed raises are not.

      Cash is the most cited cause of death. It is rarely the first problem. It is the last one.

      CB Insights, 111 post-mortems; Companies Act ROC fee schedule.

      07

      Real time is the foundation.

      The longer an entry waits, the worse everything else gets. The law already agrees.

      1. Mar 2021Audit trail rule notified
      2. Apr 2021 · Apr 2022Deferred twice
      3. 1 Apr 2023In force. Every edit logged.
      4. FY 2023–24Auditors report on it, Rule 11(g)

      Ledge 2025; Vena 2026; Companies (Accounts) Rules via TaxGuru and Deloitte India.

      08

      India is the world's next office.

      In 2024 the forecast was 2,100 to 2,200 capability centres by 2030. India got there four years early.

      Nasscom & Zinnov via BizzBuzz and YourStory; Finance Minister via KNN India.
